# Ring-verse

A pastiche of Tolkien's ring inscription, mapped onto OneEmbedding's numbers.
Drafted as a tagline; current title slide uses only the two final couplet
lines. Full version preserved here for inclusion in the README's intro.

> *Five pLMs for the model-makers under the sky,*
> *Nine Datasets for the benchmarks in their halls of test,*
> *Four Tasks for Mortal probes doomed to score,*
> *Two-hundred Methods on the Codec-thread spun,*
> *In the Land of UniProt where the Embeddings lie.*
> *One Embedding to rule them all, One Embedding to find them,*
> *One Embedding to bring them all and in the binary bind them,*
> *In the Land of UniProt where the Embeddings lie.*

The mapping:

| Tolkien | OneEmbedding |
|---|---|
| Three Rings for the Elven-kings | Five pLMs for the model-makers (ProtT5, ProstT5, ESM2, ESM-C, ANKH) |
| Seven for the Dwarf-lords in their halls of stone | Nine Datasets for the benchmarks in their halls of test |
| Nine for Mortal Men doomed to die | Four Tasks for Mortal probes doomed to score (SS3, SS8, retrieval, disorder) |
| One for the Dark Lord on his dark throne | Two-hundred Methods on the Codec-thread spun (the search) |
| In the Land of Mordor where the Shadows lie | In the Land of UniProt where the Embeddings lie |
| One Ring to rule them all, One Ring to find them | One Embedding to rule them all, One Embedding to find them |
| One Ring to bring them all and in the darkness bind them | One Embedding to bring them all and in the binary bind them |

"In the binary bind them" is the load-bearing pun: 1-bit per dimension as the
binding constraint.
